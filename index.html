I’m Simone Menoni, a Communication Designer based in Milan. Since childhood, visual arts have been my primary way to express myself and connect with others. This passion led me to study at Politecnico di Milano, where I learned to design both to solve problems and provoke reflection. I love bringing bold ideas to life through teamwork, ensuring every detail holds meaning. I enjoy learning the rules and sometimes intentionally breaking them.

Project 01

Soema

2026

Speculative Design

Interaction Design

Academic Project

Soema is a speculative design project exploring infrastructural conflict. An interactive sample collection allows users to physically touch macabre future building materials, created by recycling bodies of human victims for an exclusive showroom in 2052.

Project 02

CiviCare

2025

Metaproject

Service Design

Academic Project

CiviCare is an educational project that transforms Civic Education into an immersive role-playing game. Guided by an Artificial Intelligence, students collaborate in a narrative campaign, making collective decisions to progressively build a new society.
